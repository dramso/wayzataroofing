Don's Story

Built by someone who lives here

Don Tran is a first-generation Minnesotan, born and raised in the Lake Minnetonka area. He moved away for a time, then came back to raise his own kids in the same community that raised him. He coaches youth sports and stays involved with local associations throughout Wayzata.

After years working in the roofing industry, Don started Wayzata Roofing to do things the way homeowners actually want: straight answers, real photos of real homes, and a crew that shows up when it says it will. We are retail-focused, not storm chasers, and we treat every property, big or small, with the same level of care.
