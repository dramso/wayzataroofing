Why Plymouth roofs are aging together

Most of Plymouth went up between the early eighties and the mid two-thousands, which means a very large number of roofs reach replacement age at the same time. Whole streets off Vicksburg and Fernbrook come due within a few years of each other.

That is exactly the market storm-chasing crews target. We are retail-focused. We quote the work your roof actually needs, we tell you when it has three good years left, and we put the scope in writing either way.

We are twelve minutes away, which matters after the job as much as during it. We are not a phone number in another metro. If something needs attention two winters from now, you are calling the same company.

The Short Answers

What Plymouth homeowners ask first

Do you serve Plymouth?

Yes. Plymouth is a core service area. We are based in Wayzata, twelve minutes south, and our crews work Plymouth most weeks of the year.

How fast can you get out?

You can get a realistic price range online in under a minute. Most Plymouth addresses get an on-site visit within two business days.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, licensed and insured in the state of Minnesota. We send the certificates before any work begins, without being asked.

Do you handle insurance claims?

We do, and we meet the adjuster on site. We are retail-focused rather than storm chasers, so we will also say when a claim is not worth filing.

Who pulls the permit?

We do. Plymouth requires a building permit for a roof replacement, and we handle the application and the inspection as part of the job.

What does it cost?

It depends on square footage, pitch and material. The instant estimator gives you a real range in under a minute, no phone number required.
