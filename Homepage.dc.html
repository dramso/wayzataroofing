Welcome to Wayzata Roofing

Meet your neighbors most beloved roofing crew

Don Tran grew up in the Lake Minnetonka area and raised his own family here. After years working in the roofing industry, he started Wayzata Roofing to do things the way homeowners actually want: straight answers, real photos of real homes, and a crew that shows up when it says it will.

We are retail-focused, not storm chasers. We work with homeowners who want their roof, siding, windows or gutters done right the first time, and we treat every property, big or small, with the same level of care.

The Process

From estimate to done

Step 1

Instant Online Estimate

Answer a few questions and get a realistic price range in under a minute, no phone number required.

Step 2

Consultation & Fixed Price

Only if the pricing fits: we walk the roof, advise on materials, and hand you a fixed, written project price.

Step 3

Schedule the Work

Pick a window that suits your household. Your foreman confirms the schedule in writing before the first pallet arrives.

Step 4

Delivery

The crew arrives and the roof goes on, employed craftsmen, a dedicated foreman, and daily photo reports to your phone.

Step 5 · Optional

Maintenance & Care

Optional, and worth it: annual tune-ups, moss and debris removal, and priority scheduling after storms.
