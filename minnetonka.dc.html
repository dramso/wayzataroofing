Serving Minnetonka

Why Minnetonka homes need a local roofer

Minnetonka is a city of wooded lots and 1960s to 1980s construction. Split-levels off Minnehaha Creek, walkouts backing onto Purgatory Creek, and mature oaks dropping debris into every valley and gutter run.

Those roofs share a common problem: attics that were never vented for the insulation depth we use today. We check intake and exhaust before we quote, because the ice dam forming over your entry is usually an attic issue rather than a shingle issue.

We are ten minutes away, which matters after the job as much as during it. We are not a phone number in another metro. If something needs attention two winters from now, you are calling the same company.
